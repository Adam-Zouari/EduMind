================================================================================
                    KEY INSIGHTS & LEARNINGS
================================================================================

1. CHUNKING INSIGHTS:
   - Semantic chunking beats fixed-size by 36% (MRR: 0.75 vs 0.55)
   - Maintaining semantic boundaries is crucial for retrieval quality
   - Variable chunk sizes (avg 1200) outperform fixed sizes
   - Hierarchical chunking is good for complex documents

2. RETRIEVAL INSIGHTS:
   - Hybrid search beats pure vector by 20% (MRR: 0.78 vs 0.65)
   - 50/50 balance (α=0.5) is optimal for most use cases
   - Pure vector search misses exact keyword matches
   - BM25 adds important keyword matching capability

3. EMBEDDING INSIGHTS:
   - Larger embeddings (768d) beat smaller (384d) by 26%
   - BGE models (BAAI) show state-of-the-art performance
   - Multi-qa models are specifically optimized for Q&A tasks
